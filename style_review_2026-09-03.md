# Style review -- `manuscript/main.tex` (1,325 lines, ~10,730 words, 247 sentences)

**Scope.** Writing only. Nothing below touches the science, the statistics, or the claims; every fix is a rewrite that preserves meaning. Quotes are verbatim from `main.tex` with source line-wraps joined by single spaces; line numbers refer to the file as of 2026-09-03.

**Method.** Four independent lens referees (clarity; Physica A register, calibrated on Tiwari 2021 and Di Benedetto 2023 read in full, Cardoso 2022/2023 and Galam 2021 skimmed; scientific-writing conventions; terminology) each read all 1,325 lines. The orchestrator re-opened `main.tex` for every Major, confirmed the quote exists verbatim and the problem is real, re-counted every quantitative claim with `grep`/a sentence splitter, merged duplicates across lenses and ranked. ## Verdict on journal fit

The physics core -- Hamiltonian, Glauber dynamics, Binder cumulant, the $\lambda$ scan read as a field-to-coupling ratio, the energy decomposition -- is squarely Physica A material, and the Model section already reads like the journal. The Results, Discussion and Limitations do not: they read as a statistical-validation report crossed with a lab notebook. None of the five calibration papers uses a question heading, a Limitations section, the unit "pp", or more than a handful of p-values in prose; all use "we" (including single-author ones except Galam), "versus" not "vs.", and "Fig." in text. This manuscript has five question headings, a 615-word bold-itemized Limitations block, 107 ` -- ` asides (one per ~100 words), 40 pointers to the Supplementary Material (25 in Results), 92 sentences over 45 words (12 over 95, longest 153), and repeated in-text instructions on how a result "should be reported". The hedging is dense enough to undercut claims the data support. None of this requires restructuring: renaming headings, collapsing Results statistics into one table per subsection with one interpretive sentence, splitting the flagged sentences at their existing dashes and semicolons, and trimming Limitations to ~200 plain-prose words would bring the paper to Physica A register without changing a single claim.
