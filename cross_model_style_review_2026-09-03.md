# Cross-model style review -- IsingCR manuscript (2026-09-03, post same-model pass)

## Why this round

The same-model style review of 2026-09-03 (24 Major / 58 Minor) was applied to `main.tex` during the day; the applied state is partial. Before this round the orchestrator re-derived the mechanical state of the file: the 107 ` -- ` asides are down to 2, the five question headings and the two conversational Model headings are gone, "pp" is gone, "vs." survives only in the fig:fss caption, and "Run B" / the floating `sec:polarization-trend` label are fixed. Not applied: the Limitations bold run-ins (10 `\textbf{}` labels in one paragraph), the 16 two-decimal percentages between l.678 and l.832, the Abstract's final sentence (twin of the Conclusion opener), the Conclusion's numeric re-tabulation, the inline press citations, the 13 sentences opening with a numeral, and the "Fig." replacement -- applied to 3 of 8 figure references, so the file now alternates "Fig.~" (l.99, 163, 1015) and "Figure~" (l.473, 557, 581, 798, 929), an inconsistency the previous pass introduced. Three different model families were then given an identical hostile-copy-editor brief and told to read at least two of the five Physica A calibration papers before judging register.

## Summary assessment

The three referees agree on the diagnosis and disagree on the severity. Kimi (46 findings, 7 MAJOR) and DeepSeek-v4-pro (29 findings, 10 MAJOR) independently converge on the same four structural problems -- the headline result narrated four times in near-identical words, a Results paragraph that adjudicates its own statistics in Discussion voice, stacked hedging, and an essayistic register that keeps slipping into anthropomorphism and journalese ("headline" eight times, "does the work", "beats baseline", "gravitates toward", "find its way there", "foreshadowing") -- and both quote the same 40-word sentence (l.551--554, "Our central new finding is therefore a real, reproducible effect size...") as the worst in the paper. GLM-4.6 (24 findings, 12 MAJOR) produced only sentence-splitting and near-trivial edits, mislocated two quotes (body text labelled as figure captions), issued one finding with no verbatim quote (invalid under the brief, and factually wrong), and in its verdict sided against the other two on every register question: it called the hedging density "appropriate", the Abstract "well-structured" and the Results/Discussion/Conclusion separation "clear". Both kimi and DeepSeek judge the manuscript "not ready as it stands" but "one disciplined revision pass away" (kimi) with "physics prose often excellent" (DeepSeek); GLM judges it "substantially ready". Where the cross-model panel pushed back on the applied same-model pass -- and this is the main value of the round -- it did so in six specific places, listed under "Where the models disagreed". Eight MAJORs survived Step 3 verification; the Fable-5 second pass kept six and downgraded two (hedging density -- refuted as an aggregate by a normalized count that puts the manuscript inside the corpus range; the untranslated Spanish quotation -- real, but a two-entry fix), and amended three remedies, one because it would have strengthened a claim and two because they conflicted with the journal's own 250-word / "principal results and major conclusions" abstract rules. DeepSeek's two Spanish-accent MAJORs were downgraded on inspection of the raw data (the MIDEPLAN axis names are the source tables' own unaccented column labels; "Orosi" is a real slip but a one-character copy-edit), and its single-author-"we" MAJOR was moved to the disagreements section because the corpus evidence cuts both ways.
