# Official GAM boundary (Plan GAM 2013-2030)

- `gam_limite/GAM_LIMITE_1.*`: polygon "Limite_GAM" from MIVAH's GeoExplora open-data hub
  (https://geoexplora-mivah.opendata.arcgis.com, item 8a35a1284ade4578a1035a78468bb2f5, layer 4 "GAM LIMITE 1"),
  downloaded 2026-09-08. EPSG:5367 (CRTM05). Area 1779.79 km^2, matching the Plan GAM 2013-2030 figure and the
  coordinate box in Decreto 38334 art. 2. Redistributed here for reproducibility; source: MIVAH.
- `Division_Politico_administrativa_Limites_GAM.pdf`: MIVAH cartographic sheet I-3.1-1 "Division politico
  administrativa y limites - Plan GAM 2013" (distritos of the GAM coloured by province), from
  https://www.mivah.go.cr/Documentos/PlanGAM2013/03-CARTOGRAFIA/1_Dimension_Urbano_Regional/ .
- `Reglamento_Decreto_38334.pdf`: the Plan GAM regulation (La Gaceta 82, 30 Apr 2014).
- Per-distrito area fractions: `data/processed/gam_distrito_fraction_2026.csv` (scripts/run_gam_true_field.py).
